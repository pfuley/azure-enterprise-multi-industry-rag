Enterprise RAG systems retrieve relevant information from trusted documents before generating an answer. Retrieval-Augmented Generation, or RAG, is an artificial intelligence framework that enhances large language models by pulling relevant facts from external data sources before generating a response. Instead of relying solely on its static internal memory, the system searches a targeted knowledge base for specific documents related to the user prompt. It then combines those retrieved text snippets with the original query to provide accurate, up-to-date, and context-aware answers. This external lookup step significantly reduces false information and hallucinations while keeping the underlying model grounded in factual data. Organizations frequently use this method to connect private enterprise files or live databases to conversational agents securely. Developers can update the searchable knowledge pool at any time without retraining the entire neural network. Ultimately, this hybrid approach delivers smarter, more reliable, and verifiable outputs for complex search and support tasks.
